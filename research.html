Dr. Luca Brunke

Postdoctoral Researcher · University of Augsburg

Current research

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Augsburg, in the group for Image Processing & Visualization in the Digital Humanities. My work develops computational methods for cultural heritage: photogrammetric and laser-scan reconstruction, semantic data modelling, and the research infrastructure that connects capture to interpretation.

My doctorate, undertaken at the University of Exeter with The National Gallery, London, examined digital reconstruction as a method in art-historical and archaeological enquiry. That thread continues in my current work bridging heritage documentation and scholarship.
