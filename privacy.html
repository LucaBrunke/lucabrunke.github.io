Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung nach DSGVO · This site is built to collect as little personal data as possible.

01

Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is Luca Brunke, Augsburg, Germany. Full contact details are given in the Imprint.

02

Overview

This is a static personal website. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies, uses no analytics that profile visitors, and asks for no login. Fonts and the 3D viewer are served from this site itself, not from third-party servers. Personal data is processed only where technically unavoidable (server logs) or where you actively contact me.

03

Server log files

When you visit the site, the hosting provider automatically records technical data such as your IP address, the requested page, referrer, browser type and time of access. This is necessary to deliver the site securely and reliably (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest).

Log data is not merged with other data sources and is deleted after a short retention period defined by the hosting provider.

04

Hosting & CDN

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation), 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, and delivered via its Fastly CDN. GitHub processes connection data (such as your IP address) on my behalf as a processor under its standard Data Protection Addendum, which is incorporated into GitHub’s Terms of Service and applies automatically to all GitHub Pages sites, without a separate signature.

As GitHub is based in the United States, this involves a transfer of data outside the EU/EEA. GitHub’s Data Protection Addendum incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses to safeguard this transfer.

05

Contact by email

This site has no contact form. If you email me, your message and address are processed solely to respond to your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed.

06

Embedded 3D & external content

Interactive 3D models may load a viewer library and model files. Where content is embedded from a third party (e.g. a model host such as Sketchfab), that provider may receive your IP address. Such content is loaded only as needed to display the work.

07

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Bavaria, this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at the address in the Imprint.
